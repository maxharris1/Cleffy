moonlight1.mid, moonlight2.mid, and moonlight3.mid are the three movement
MIDI files of the Mutopia Project edition of Beethoven, Piano Sonata
Op. 27 No. 2 (“Moonlight”), arranged by Stewart Holmes.

Source: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BeethovenLv/O27/moonlight/
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.5
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/

The matching LilyPond A4 PDF (`moonlight-a4.pdf`) is not vendored in this
repository. The recorded baseline was produced from that PDF.
