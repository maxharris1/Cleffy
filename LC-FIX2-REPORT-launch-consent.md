## Open questions

1. **`resend-inbound` forwarding is outside the shared budget.** It sends one mail to our own mailbox per inbound message, and anyone can mail support@, so it is aimable.
    - It predates this round and is on `dev`. The cross-PR rule is "no email goes out except through the shared mail budget".
    - Registering it would need a seventh category in the locked `LAUNCH_MAIL_SPLIT`, and no branch may define its own numbers. So I did not change it.
    - **Max to decide:** add an `inboundForward` share (taking room from another category) in checkout's canonical file, or exempt it explicitly because it goes only to our own address.
2. **District attribution rule.** A student counts as the district's when the teacher who provisioned them owned, or was seated in, a district studio at that moment. That includes a student a seated teacher provisions for what they consider private work, while seated.
    - Students provisioned before a teacher joined, or after they left, are never the district's.
    - Pre-existing rows are backfilled only from a seat held since before the student was created. A departed teacher's students from before this migration stay unattributed, and so are never purged by a district, unless corrected with `admin_set_student_district()`.
    - **Max to confirm** this matches the contract terms, or say whether provisioning should carry an explicit district choice instead.
3. **District notices wake on the daily run only.** The sender is woken by `retention_district_run` (daily, still unscheduled by design). With 5/hour, a day with more than 5 district notices spreads them over following runs, and the purge for those districts waits accordingly. That is safe, but slow.
    - **Max to decide** whether to also schedule the sender hourly once the cron is enabled.
4. **Expected textual conflict in `src/sync/syncEngine.ts` when #64 is rebased after #63.** Session B reports that #63 adds a watermark hold (`noteGap()`) in `applyServerRow` and `pullOnce`; Max approved it, scoped to #63's sync gating. When resolving, keep both conditions on the broadcast path:
    - #63's hold check;
    - #64's gate, which advances only once `state.checkedSeq !== undefined`, with the `...state` / `checkedSeq` spread.
      The two are complementary: #63's hold is re-armed per gap, while #64's gate covers the legacy-state upgrade. Re-run `src/sync/syncEngine.test.ts` after the merge; it has both suites.
5. **No full local `db reset` this round.** See "Checks run" for exactly what was verified. Max may want one fresh apply of the merged set after `npm run migrations:mirror`.
